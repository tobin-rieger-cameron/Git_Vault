# Taxonomy of Human Knowledge

## Table of Contents
### 1 Introduction

The major top-level branches of human knowledge can be broadly categorized into several disciplines, each with its unique approach to understanding and analyzing complex systems.

### 2 The Five Branches of Human Knowledge

#### Natural Sciences
*   Fields: physics, chemistry, biology, earth sciences, astronomy
*   Study the natural world through empirical evidence and experimentation

The natural sciences encompass a wide range of fields that study the natural world. From the intricate mechanisms of living organisms to the vast expanse of the universe, these disciplines strive to understand the underlying laws and principles that govern our existence.

#### Social Sciences
*   Fields: sociology, psychology, anthropology, economics, politics
*   Examine human social behavior, relationships, and institutions

The social sciences delve into the complexities of human interaction and society. By studying human behavior, relationships, and institutions, these disciplines aim to comprehend the underlying dynamics that shape our world.

#### Humanities
*   Fields: literature, history, philosophy, art, music, languages
*   Focus on the study of human culture, expression, and experience

The humanities are concerned with the study of human culture, expression, and experience. These disciplines explore the rich tapestry of human creativity, from the written word to visual arts, music, and language.

#### Formal Sciences
*   Fields: mathematical logic, computer science, formal languages
*   Use rigorous methods to analyze and reason about abstract systems

The formal sciences employ rigorous methods to analyze and reason about abstract systems. By leveraging mathematical logic and computational power, these disciplines aim to uncover the underlying structures that govern complex phenomena.

#### Applied Sciences
*   Fields: engineering, agriculture, medicine, environmental sciences
*   Apply theoretical knowledge from other disciplines to solve practical problems

The applied sciences bring together theoretical knowledge from various disciplines to address real-world challenges. By applying rigorous methods and empirical evidence, these fields aim to develop innovative solutions that improve our lives.

### 3 Overlap and Nuances

While the five branches of human knowledge provide a useful framework for understanding complex systems, there is often overlap between them. For instance, a historian may draw upon concepts from sociology and psychology to better comprehend historical events.
