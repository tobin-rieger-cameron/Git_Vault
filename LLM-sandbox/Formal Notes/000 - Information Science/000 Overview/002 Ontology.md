>In [[000 Information Science|information science]], an ontology is a formal representation of the names, categories, and relationships between concepts, data, or entities that belong to a given subject. 

Any formal discipline or field, once described, lays the groundwork for an ontology. Each could use ontological assumptions to frame explicit theories, research, and applications. 

What ontologies in both information science and [[Philosophy]] have in common is the attempt to represent entities, including both objects and events, with all their interdependent properties and relations, according to a system of categories. There is considerable work on problems of ontology engineering
