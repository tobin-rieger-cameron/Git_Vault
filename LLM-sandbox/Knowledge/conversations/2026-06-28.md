What are the three stages of RLHF and what are the main alternatives to PPO?

## [11:28] Assistant

The three stages of RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback) are:

1. **Stage 1: Reward Model Training**: In this stage, a reward model is trained on human-annotated data. The reward model assigns a score to the output of the language model based on how well it aligns with human preferences.
2. **Stage 2: Score-to-Preference Mapping**: This stage involves training a mapping function that converts the scores from the reward model into preference labels (e.g., "good" or "bad").
3. **Stage 3: RL Optimisation (PPO)**: In this final stage, the language model is fine-tuned using the PPO algorithm to maximize the reward score. The policy is the language model being trained, and the reward signal comes from the reward model.

As for alternatives to PPO, there are several methods that have been proposed:

* **DPO (Direct Preference Optimization)**: This method skips the explicit reward model and RL entirely by optimising a reparameterised objective directly on preference pairs. It is simpler and more stable than traditional PPO.
* **RLAIF (AI Feedback)**: Instead of using human labellers, RLAIF uses a stronger AI model to generate preference labels. This approach is cheaper to scale than traditional PPO.
* **Constitutional AI**: This method uses a set of principles ("the constitution") and AI self-critique to generate preference data. The model critiques and revises its own outputs before human labellers review.
* **KTO (Kahneman-Tversky Optimisation)**: This approach uses individual human ratings rather than pairwise comparisons, making it more data-efficient.

These alternatives aim to simplify or modify the traditional PPO algorithm to improve efficiency, scalability, and stability in RLHF training.
