# What is Faceted Search and How Do Large Retailers and Libraries Use It to Make Huge Catalogs Easy to Browse?

Faceted search is a powerful tool that enables users to navigate and explore vast collections of information by breaking down complex categorizations into multiple, independent attributes or facets. This approach differs from traditional hierarchical classification systems, which often rely on a single linear dimension to organize content.

## What is Faceted Search?

At its core, faceted search involves assigning objects or concepts to categories based on multiple facets, allowing users to filter and browse through the collection using various combinations of these attributes. For instance, in an ecommerce context, a user might navigate through products by simultaneously filtering for size, color, brand, and price.

## How Do Large Retailers and Libraries Use Faceted Search?

Large retailers and libraries leverage faceted search to make their massive catalogs more accessible and user-friendly. By exposing facets as sidebar filters or interactive interfaces, they enable users to quickly drill down into the most relevant information without having to perform a traditional search.

One notable example of faceted classification in action is the Colon Classification system developed by S.R. Ranganathan in the 1930s, which defined five universal facets: Personality (primary subject), Matter (material or substance), Energy (process or activity), Space (geographic focus), and Time (period). This foundational theory laid the groundwork for modern faceted classification systems.

## Faceted Filtering

Large retailers often adopt a variant of faceted search known as "faceted filtering," where users can simultaneously filter through multiple attributes to narrow down their results. For example, in an ecommerce context, a user might use filters such as size, color, brand, and price to quickly find the most relevant products.

## Examples of Faceted Classification

- **Ranganathan's Colon Classification** — Colon Classification is a canonical faceted library system.
- **BISAC subject codes** — BISAC subject codes are publishing industry categories that use multiple axes for classification.
- **Ecommerce filtering** — many modern ecommerce platforms use faceted filtering to enable users to browse products by multiple attributes.
- **Faceted search interfaces** — most modern search UIs expose facets as sidebar filters, enabling users to quickly filter and browse through large collections of information.
