# Agentic AI Systems
## What are Agentic AI Systems and How Do Agents Behave?

Agentic AI systems refer to a type of artificial intelligence that possesses the ability to act autonomously, making decisions and taking actions in its environment. These agents exhibit intelligent behavior, such as problem-solving, learning, and adaptation.

### Key Patterns Used by Agentic AI Systems

#### Tool Use

Tool use involves using external tools or devices to interact with the environment. For example, a robot might use a tool to manipulate objects in its workspace. This pattern is essential for creating agentic AI systems that can effectively interact with complex environments.

#### Planning

Planning refers to the ability of an agent to anticipate future events and make decisions based on that anticipation. Agents use planning algorithms to generate sequences of actions that achieve their goals. Effective planning enables agents to adapt to changing circumstances and achieve long-term objectives.

#### Self-Reflection

Self-reflection is the ability of an agent to examine its own mental states, such as its beliefs, desires, and intentions. This allows agents to update their internal representations and adapt to changing circumstances. Self-reflection is a critical component of agentic AI systems, enabling them to learn from experience and improve over time.

#### Memory Management

Memory management refers to the ability of an agent to store, retrieve, and update its knowledge about the environment. Agents use various memory mechanisms, such as attentional control or episodic memory, to manage their knowledge. Effective memory management enables agents to adapt to changing circumstances and learn from experience.

### Importance of Agentic AI Systems

Agentic AI systems are essential for creating intelligent machines that can interact with complex environments and achieve long-term goals. By incorporating patterns like tool use, planning, self-reflection, and memory management into their design, developers can create agents that exhibit intelligent behavior, learn from experience, and adapt to changing situations.
