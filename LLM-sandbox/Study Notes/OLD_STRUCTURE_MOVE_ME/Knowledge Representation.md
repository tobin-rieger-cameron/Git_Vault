>Addresses surrogates of knowledge and of documents in information systems. data describing documents and knowledge, often called [[Knowledge Representation|metadata]]. 

Added to this are methods and tools for use during indexing and summarization. 
Essential tools are Knowledge Organization Systems (KOSs), which provide concepts and designations for indexing and retrieval. 

The fundamental question is: how can knowledge in digital systems be represented, organized and condensed in such a way that it can be retrieved as easily as possible?
