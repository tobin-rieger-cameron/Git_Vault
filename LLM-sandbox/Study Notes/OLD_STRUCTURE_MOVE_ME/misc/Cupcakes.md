# Individual Sized Cakes

## Q: what are cupcakes?
*Source: model knowledge — 2026-06-24 11:17*

Cupcakes are individual-sized cakes that are typically baked in a muffin tin and decorated with frosting and various toppings. They often have a rounded or domed shape and are usually smaller than a full-sized cake. Cupcakes can be flavored in a wide range of ways, from classic vanilla and chocolate to more unique flavors like red velvet or carrot cake.
