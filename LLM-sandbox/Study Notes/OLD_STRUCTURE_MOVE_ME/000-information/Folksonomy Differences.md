# Folksonomy
## Understanding the Difference from Taxonomy

Folksonomy is a term used to describe the use of tags or keywords to categorize and organize information on the web. This approach differs significantly from traditional taxonomy, which relies on controlled vocabulary and centralized authority.

### Decentralized vs. Centralized

In contrast to taxonomic systems, folksonomy often exists in a decentralized manner. Anyone can assign tags to a piece of content, allowing for greater flexibility and user participation. In contrast, taxonomy is typically centralized, with authority figures controlling the terms used to describe information.

### Flexible vs. Rigid

Folksonomy also allows for more flexibility and variations in terminology compared to taxonomy. This is because folksonomic systems often rely on community contributions and user-generated content, rather than rigid adherence to a controlled vocabulary.

### Community-driven vs. Expert-driven

Another key difference between folksonomy and taxonomy is the role of expertise. In contrast to taxonomic systems, which are typically driven by experts or authority figures, folksonomy relies on community participation and user-generated content.

## Key Concepts

*   **Decentralization**: The dispersal of power and control across a network or system, allowing for greater flexibility and autonomy.
*   **Taxonomy**: A hierarchical classification system used to organize and categorize objects or concepts based on their relationships and characteristics.
*   **Folksonomy**: A decentralized and community-driven approach to categorizing and organizing information using tags or keywords.
