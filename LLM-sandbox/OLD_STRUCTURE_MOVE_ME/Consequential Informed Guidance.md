# Constitutional AI
### Explaining Explainable and Accountable AI through Principles

Constitutional AI is a type of artificial intelligence that aims to create more explainable, accountable, and transparent models by incorporating a set of principles or "constitution" into their design. These principles serve as guidelines for the model's behavior during training, ensuring it adheres to certain values and norms.

## Key Concepts
### Principles Guiding Constitutional AI

In constitutional AI, the principles are typically drawn from ethical frameworks, such as those proposed by the IEEE Global Initiative on Ethics of Autonomous and Intelligent Systems [[IEEE Framework]]. These principles might include:

*   **Transparency**: The model should provide clear explanations for its decisions and actions.
*   **Fairness**: The model should treat all individuals equally and without bias.
*   **Explainability**: The model should be able to explain its reasoning and decision-making process.

## Key Concepts
### Techniques Used in Constitutional AI

Constitutional AI can involve various techniques, such as:

1.  **Value-aligned design**: The model's architecture and training objectives are designed to align with the principles.
2.  **Regularization techniques**: Techniques like L1 or L2 regularization can be used to encourage model behavior that aligns with the constitution.
3.  **Adversarial training**: Adversarial examples can be used to test the model's robustness and ensure it adheres to the principles.

## Key Concepts
### The Goal of Constitutional AI

By incorporating a constitution into AI development, constitutional AI aims to create more responsible and accountable systems that prioritize human values and well-being.
