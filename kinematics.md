# Kinematics

## Q: what is kinematics?
*Source: model knowledge — 2026-06-24 11:24*

Kinematics is the branch of physics that studies the motion of objects without considering the forces that cause the motion. It focuses on describing the motion of an object in terms of its position, velocity, and acceleration as a function of time.

In other words, kinematics deals with the study of how things move, but not why they move. It provides a description of an object's motion using mathematical equations, graphs, and formulas, without taking into account the forces that influence the motion.

Kinematics is a fundamental concept in physics, engineering, and many other fields, as it helps to understand and predict the motion of objects in various contexts, such as projectile motion, circular motion, and linear motion.
