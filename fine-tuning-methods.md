## Summary
Fine-tuning is a process of retraining pre-trained large language models on smaller datasets without access to online computing resources. This approach is often done offline, using cached versions of the training data.

## Key Concepts

*   **Large Language Models (LLMs)**: Pre-trained models that have been trained on vast amounts of text data and can be fine-tuned for specific tasks or datasets.
*   **Fine-Tuning**: The process of retraining a pre-trained LLM on smaller, labeled datasets without online access to computing resources.

## Main Approaches for Fine-Tuning

### 1. Increasing Training Data

Providing more diverse and high-quality training data can significantly improve the performance of a pre-trained LLM.
[[Increasing Training Data](../increasing-training-data)]

### 2. Fine-tuning with Specific Tasks

Fine-tuning a pre-trained LLM on specific tasks or datasets can help improve its performance on those tasks.

### 3. Adjusting Hyperparameters

Experimenting with different hyperparameters, such as learning rate, batch size, and number of epochs, can also improve the performance of a language model.
[[Adjusting Hyperparameters](../adjusting-hyperparameters)]
